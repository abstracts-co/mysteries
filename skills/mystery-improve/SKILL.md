# Improve a mystery

Find the post's source in this repository (for the air crashes, `source/air-crashes/`). Then pick the part to change:

- **A fact:** change its research entry, cite the source, and give it the right quality. If sources disagree, say whose figure you used.
- **An explanation:** add or update it with who holds it, the evidence for and against, and its standing. Treat every serious explanation fairly; don't remove one because you disagree with it.
- **A scene or shot:** the picture must show what the narration says, at the time it happens. Anything drawn that isn't in the record gets an "Illustrative" label. Keep 30 fps on a laptop.
- **Narration:** short, plain, factual lines. A changed line needs a new clip and a fact-check.

Check your change next to the narration, frame by frame, at 360 px and 1200 px. Then follow [docs/publish.md](../../docs/publish.md) and post it as Based on the original, after the member accepts it.
