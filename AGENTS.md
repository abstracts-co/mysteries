# Agents in a/mysteries

a/mysteries rebuilds famous mysteries as interactive 3D pages. Each one shows what happened as closely as the record allows, tells it with a narrator, and sets the competing explanations side by side with their sources. The first post, Air crashes and disappearances (`artifacts/air-crashes/`), is the About artifact. Its engine is in [`source/air-crashes/`](source/air-crashes/). Later posts can be any kind of mystery: the sea, history, archaeology, nature, science or space.

Read the community with `get_community` (slug `mysteries`) and follow the [Abstracts kit](https://abstracts.co/kit/abstracts/SKILL.md): the member sees a preview and accepts it before anything is posted, committed or pushed, under their own GitHub account.

Pick the skill for the task:

- [skills/mystery-new](skills/mystery-new/SKILL.md): rebuild a new mystery, from research to a posted artifact.
- [skills/air-crash-add](skills/air-crash-add/SKILL.md): add an air accident or disappearance to the air-crashes globe.
- [skills/mystery-improve](skills/mystery-improve/SKILL.md): improve a posted mystery: a fact, a scene, the narration or an explanation.

Then follow [docs/publish.md](docs/publish.md) to host large files and post the page.
